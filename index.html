My Unofficial Local Food Guide to Washington Ave- BK (Prospect Heights)

Gen Resturant

Gen is a Japanese Resturant that has been on my block since I was born, offering up many a birthday dinners as I have never been a birthday party person. At gen they offer up a ton of high end dishes, however I have always loved gen for the Udon Noodle Soup which comes with shrimp and vegtable tempura. Other items that are worth mentioning is their sashimi and eel served with rice and vegtables. The eel is coated in the sweet and savory sauce that is perfectly mixed between thick and thin consistency. As far as Gen's contribution to the neighborhood, it has beautiful outdoor seating and they usually have some elaborate drawing on the sidewalk to greet neighbors.



Cheryl's Global Soul Food

Cheryl's Global Soul food is actually a resturant that has become a staple to our neighborhood with the owner, Cheryl, being extremley adventerous to incorperating multiple cuisines. She is also extremley sweet so she is a bit of a local celebrity. I personally think the fried catfish with fries is extremley worth the hype, along with other dishes like the korean bbq fried chicken and the chicken and pancakes. Its location is tucked away on St. Marks but with recent fads like Radio Bakery that opened up a block away from it, it is activley gaining more traction.



Panca BK

Panca BK is a extremely expensive resturant I have been to once but I wanted to add it if anyone is bougie or looking for a place to spend all their money. This is a South-American/ Mediterrainian resturant, and it definitley has a bit of somthing for evryone. For me, the octopus is my favorite dish. Even though its technically a starter it is maybe the best thing I have ever eaten, or at least in the top 3. Because they have a wood-fired grill a lot of the dishes have a smokey taste, including the grilled whole fish which i love. Overall I think all their seafood is top notch.
